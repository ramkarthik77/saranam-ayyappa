01 The divine story

Who is Lord Ayyappa?

Revered as Dharma Sastha, Hariharaputra and Manikandan, Lord Ayyappa embodies dharma, self-mastery and devotion. Explore the story as preserved in Sabarimala temple tradition.

THE SON OF HARI AND HARA

A divine child.
An eternal teaching.

In the devotional narrative, Lord Shiva and Vishnu as Mohini are the divine parents of Ayyappa. King Rajasekhara of Pandalam raises the child Manikandan, whose journey leads to the sacred shrine at Sabarimala.

07 Questions from seekers

Before you begin.

What does Ayyappa Deeksha mean?+

It is a devotional observance emphasizing prayer, restraint, simplicity and discipline. The Sabarimala pilgrimage tradition commonly describes a 41-day Mandala Vratham.

Must I follow exactly the same customs as every other devotee?+

Core temple guidance is shared, but details can differ by region, family and Guru Swamy. Seek informed, respectful guidance instead of assuming every local custom is universal.

Can a first-time devotee undertake the journey?+

First-time pilgrims are often called Kanni Swamis. Traditionally, a Guru Swamy helps them understand the Mala, Vratham, Irumudi and pilgrimage responsibilities.

What is the purpose of Sarana Ghosha?+

Invocations such as “Swamiye Saranam Ayyappa” express reverence and surrender. Devotees repeat them during prayer and pilgrimage.

What should I do if I have a health or practical constraint?+

Speak with your Guru Swamy about the religious aspects, and seek qualified medical advice for health-related needs. Do not compromise health or safety to follow an online checklist.
